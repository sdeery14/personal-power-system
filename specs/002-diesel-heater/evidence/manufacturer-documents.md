# Manufacturer documents on record — provenance and page findings (M1, M2, M3)

**Status:** provenance record for the VEVOR documents cited in [spec.md §2](../spec.md#2-manufacturer-sources), with the page-specific findings of the 2026-10-30 reading of the newly supplied manual (M3) and its comparison with the earlier manual (M1). Everything here is **Manufacturer-stated** unless tagged otherwise. A manufacturer statement is not a verification, a clearance, a load rating, or an operating authorization for this module; how each statement applies to an open T-slot frame is an engineering question recorded in [design.md](../design.md). G1–G5 in [spec.md §10](../spec.md#10-release-gates) stay closed.

## 4. What this record does not do

- It does not make M3 the manual supplied with the owner's unit (Q-01, DOC-01, DOC-02) or settle the model mapping (Q-12).
- It does not turn any M3 figure into a clearance for this frame: the 10 cm, 4 in, and 2 in figures are the manufacturer's installation instructions for vehicle and building installations, and how they apply to an open T-slot frame is recorded as an engineering question in design.md §5 and §8.
- It does not authorize drilling the tank's nozzle position, opening the three mounting passages, purchasing, fabrication, power connection, or operation.
- It does not change any gate; G1–G5 remain closed.
