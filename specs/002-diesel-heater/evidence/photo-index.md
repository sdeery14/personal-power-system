## 3. Observations worth carrying forward (still Visually observed, not Measured)

1. **Plate orientation on the heater (PLT-14).** E-03 and E-04 show the shorter plate dimension running along the heater's length and the longer dimension across it. This is consistent with the CAD placing the 5 7/16 in side along the heater (`PLATE_ALONG`) and deriving the crossmember spacing from that side's hole pitch. It does not replace the PLT-14 observation in a dated record, which also fixes the L and T axis aliases for every HTR row.
2. **Two interfaces are visibly distinct (D-16).** The four nutted studs clustered around the stubs (E-01, E-02) are not the four corner holes. This supports treating Interface A (heater to plate) and Interface B (plate to frame) as separate, as [design.md §5](../design.md#5-heater-mounting) already does. It does not show how the heater's load reaches the plate, whether the studs are the only Interface A fixings, or whether corner-hole bolts can be fitted with the heater seated.
3. **Central features sit inside the corner-hole rectangle (E-01).** The stubs, studs, slot, and small fitting are all visibly between the two corner-hole lines in both directions. The margin between them and a 20 mm crossmember strip at either pitch cannot be read from a photograph; that is the §5 dry-fit check (PLT-10, PLT-11, HTR-12 to HTR-14).
4. **The tank photographs cover one face only.** Three recesses are visible; the two close-ups show centres of different appearance, one dark (E-08) and one metallic-looking with concentric marks (E-09). This neither confirms nor resolves the owner's membrane report (2026-10-27) and through-hole report (2026-10-28); it adds a third possibility, a metal insert, that only inspection of each point (TNK-10 to TNK-12) can settle.
5. **Not photographed at all:** the tank outlet (TNK-15), the tank's other face, edges, top, and bottom; the plate's underside and opening (PLT-10, PLT-11); the heater base with the plate removed; any seal (HTR-16, PLT-12); the tank accessory kit (TNK-16); the fuel pump, filter, and in-line fuse (PMP, FLT, ELC-02); the controller and harness.
