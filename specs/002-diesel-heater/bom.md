### 2.1 Supplier compatibility criteria (evidence to obtain before G1)

Profiles sold as "2020" differ between suppliers in slot width, wall thickness, alloy, and the bracket and T-nut families that fit them. **Current proposed package (Owner decision 2026-10-30): TNUTZ only**, EXM-2020 extrusion custom-cut to the metric lengths with TNUTZ CBK-020-A corner-bracket kits and DB-020-M5 T-nuts (§2.2). *Historical:* on 2026-10-29 the package was first an all-ZYLtech family and then a hybrid of TNUTZ extrusion with ZYLtech hardware, which made the fit between the two suppliers' parts an item of pre-purchase evidence in its own right; with one supplier that question no longer arises (the superseded row is kept below, marked). The following are to be obtained and recorded in design.md before G1(e) is reviewed. None of it is assumed. The status column records what the supplier pages stated when read on 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30; "not stated" means a supplier inquiry, a drawing, or a measured sample is still needed.

**Pre-purchase basis versus post-delivery check.** TNUTZ states (FAQ, read 2026-10-29) that it does not accept returns on extrusions because they are all custom cut, and that extrusion orders cannot be cancelled or refunded. A custom-cut TNUTZ order is therefore **nonreturnable**, and whatever the joint hardware depends on has to be settled **before** that order is placed, as part of the "compatible hardware" of G1(e), not after delivery. With a single supplier the available pre-purchase basis is the supplier's own classification: the CBK-020-A kit is listed under "20 Series / 6mm Slot" and the DB-020-M5 nut is stated "fully compatible" with TNUTZ extrusions (Manufacturer-stated, read 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30). That is a statement of fit by category, not a load rating and not a dimensional drawing; it is the basis the owner may accept in writing at G1(e). Inspecting one assembled joint after delivery is an **acceptance check**: it confirms that the delivered parts behave as the pre-purchase basis said they would. It cannot substitute for that basis, because by then the extrusion cannot be returned.
