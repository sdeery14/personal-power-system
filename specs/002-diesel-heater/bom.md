### 2.1 Supplier compatibility criteria (evidence to obtain before G1)

Profiles sold as "2020" differ between suppliers in slot width, wall thickness, alloy, and the bracket and T-nut families that fit them. **Current proposed package (Owner decision 2026-10-30): TNUTZ only**, EXM-2020 extrusion custom-cut to the metric lengths with TNUTZ CBK-020-A corner-bracket kits and DB-020-M5 T-nuts (§2.2). *Historical:* on 2026-10-29 the package was first an all-ZYLtech family and then a hybrid of TNUTZ extrusion with ZYLtech hardware, which made the fit between the two suppliers' parts an item of pre-purchase evidence in its own right; with one supplier that question no longer arises (the superseded row is kept below, marked). The relevant full G1(e) mounting assessments must be documented before their respective fasteners are purchased, but **G1-P does not require those mounting-screw dimensions**. Supplier documentation, justified engineering analysis, **or measured delivered loose parts after G1-P (under an approved G2-D when applicable)** may supply that evidence. Unresolved facts stay Unknown. The supplier-page status below records what was stated when read on 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30.

**G1-P / full G1 distinction:** For a structural-only G1-P purchase, the already documented single-supplier part-family compatibility is the bounded procurement basis; unknown bracket strength, unverified plate/tank screws, nut depth and slot-lip dimensions remain explicit risks and are **not resolved by purchase**. The dimensional basis for final mounting-screw lengths and boss adequacy is still required under **full G1** before selecting those screws. Supplier custom cutting is intrinsic to a G1-P order; owner-side assembly and loading require subsequent approval (G2-D or full G2 as applicable).

**Pre-purchase basis versus post-delivery check.** TNUTZ states (FAQ, read 2026-10-29) that it does not accept returns on extrusions because they are all custom cut, and that extrusion orders cannot be cancelled or refunded. A custom-cut TNUTZ order is therefore **nonreturnable**. The proposed G1-P release permits a **consciously risk-accepted structural-only order** on Manufacturer-stated 20 Series fit evidence; it does **not** require final tank/plate screw sizing or MR-05/G2 structural approval before supplier cutting. The dimensional bases and adequate engagement of those **separately purchased mounting fasteners** remain required before their full G1 purchase, and delivered fit is assessed at G2-D/G2 as appropriate. With a single supplier the available pre-purchase basis is the supplier's own compatibility statement: the EXM-2020 page states "All of our 20 Series T-Nuts and components are compatible with this extrusion" (<https://www.tnutz.com/product/exm-2020/>, read 2026-10-30), the CBK-020-A kit is listed under "20 Series / 6mm Slot", and the DB-020-M5 nut is stated "fully compatible" with TNUTZ extrusions (Manufacturer-stated, read 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30). That is **Manufacturer-stated compatibility evidence** (fit of the parts in the slot); it is not verified joint strength, load capacity, or racking stiffness, and not a dimensional drawing. It is the limited fit basis the owner may accept in writing at **G1-P**, while full G1(e)/G2 still require load-path and installation adequacy decisions. Inspecting an unloaded structural bracket joint after delivery is a **structural-package compatibility check** against the supplier family-fit evidence accepted for G1-P; it cannot retroactively justify the nonreturnable structural order. **Separately, delivered loose T-nut/slot dimensions can be used to specify mounting screws for a later full G1 purchase.** Full G2 still requires installed-fit and load-path acceptance.
